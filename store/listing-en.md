## Description

Speak Bangla, and the words appear in whatever box you are typing in: a chat, an email, a search bar, a form, an AI prompt.

Ukti is for the many people who speak Bangla fluently but find typing its script slow or hard.

WHAT IT DOES
• Speak to type. Click in any text box, press the microphone (or Ctrl+Shift+Space), and speak Bangla. Your words land right where the cursor is.
• Bangla or English. Choose to get Bangla script, or have what you say translated into English as it is written.
• Banglish to Bangla. Typed "ami banglay kotha boli"? Press অ (or Alt+Shift+1) and it becomes আমি বাংলায় কথা বলি.
• Make it English. Press EN (or Alt+Shift+2) to turn the text in the box into English.
• Undo. One press (or Alt+Shift+3) puts back what was there before.
• Your shortcuts, your keys. Change any of them at chrome://extensions/shortcuts. They do nothing unless you are in a text box, and the defaults stay clear of keys Chrome and common editors use.
• Off where you do not want it: for one site, or everywhere, from the toolbar button.
• Bangla or English interface.

YOUR PRIVACY, PLAINLY
• Speech recognition is Chrome's own, and Chrome sends the audio to Google's servers to turn it into text. Ukti only listens while the microphone button is red.
• Translation runs on your computer with Chrome's built-in translator. Banglish conversion runs inside the extension.
• Ukti has no server, no account, no analytics and no ads, and its own code sends nothing anywhere.
• Password, one-time code, PIN and card fields are skipped.
• Full policy: [privacy policy URL]

WHY CHROME WARNS ABOUT "ALL WEBSITES"
The control has to be able to appear in any text box on any site, so its script runs on every page. It reads only the box you are typing in, and only when you speak into it or press one of its buttons.

REQUIREMENTS
Chrome 138 or newer on a computer, and a microphone. On first install a setup page opens: allow the microphone and download the two language packs, once.
